About

A student who loves seeing data come alive

I'm Jasfher Pantoja, a third-year Computer Science student. My interest in data started in Grade 12, when I watched a school database demo and saw the data I typed in appear as real output. That moment made me want to work with data for a living.

Since second year, I've been teaching myself data analytics. I download datasets from Kaggle, clean them, design the data model, and turn them into dashboards. I enjoy tools that involve coding, and I focus on the reasoning behind every step so I can apply it to new problems.

My goal is to become a Data Engineer. I'm starting as a Data Analyst to build a strong foundation, and I'm looking for an on-the-job training (OJT) opportunity in a data-related role.
